# What If You Bought Your House in 2006 Instead of 2012?

File: `EP07_House_2006_vs_2012.mp4` · Short: `EP07_House_2006_vs_2012_SHORT.mp4`

## Description
Same house, three versions of you. One buys at the 2006 peak and ends up underwater. One waits until 2012 and pays almost half per month. One buys the same house today and pays almost $3,000 a month. Real home price index and real mortgage rates.

Data: S&P CoreLogic Case-Shiller US National Home Price Index (FRED CSUSHPINSA), Freddie Mac 30-year fixed mortgage rate (FRED MORTGAGE30US). Example house $300,000 in Jul 2006, 20% down, 30-year fixed, principal and interest only. Taxes, insurance and maintenance not included; local markets differ.
This video is for education and entertainment only. It is not financial advice.
