# What If You Hid $10,000 Under Your Mattress in 2000?

File: `EP04_Mattress_Gold_Stocks_2000.mp4` · Short: `EP04_Mattress_Gold_Stocks_2000_SHORT.mp4`

## Description
Three versions of you hide the same $10,000 in January 2000: under the mattress, in gold, and in an S&P 500 index fund. Nobody touches it for 26 years. One loses half its value without losing a single bill. One grows over 15x. And then we change the start date...

Data: US CPI-U (BLS via FRED CPIAUCSL), euro area HICP (Eurostat via FRED), monthly gold price (github.com/datasets/gold-prices), S&P 500 monthly average price (Robert Shiller data, price only, dividends excluded). No fees, taxes or storage costs.
This video is for education and entertainment only. It is not financial advice. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
