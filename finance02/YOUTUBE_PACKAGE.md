## Опис
What if you skipped one $5 coffee a day and invested it instead, starting in January 2010?
Two timelines, same person, same $5. One ends with zero. The other ends with about $99,172.

Using real S&P 500 prices, we follow $152 a month through the 2020 crash, the 2022 drop,
what happens if you panic and sell, what if you start late, and why the "latte factor" is
not really about coffee.
